**National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda AskMe-chatbot**

We designed an AI powered chatbot which can answer different questions of user about NISR data.

Instead of manually downloading excel files from NISR website and do your own analysis AI will do analysis for you and answer different questions you have about the statistical data of our country.

Wether you are a researcher or a courious explorer, you can chat about your questions and you will get clear data-backed answers.
